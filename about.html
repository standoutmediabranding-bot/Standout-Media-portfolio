Who we are

Anyone can make a video look cool. Making it perform is the hard part. We are a 6-member creative team in Tamil Nadu, and part of Standout Media, a content marketing agency. For a long time we have lived inside the content space, helping creators, founders and startups, from local to overseas, get seen.

Why we started

Living there taught us what editing software never will: how content really works. We learned to read it like analysts, to see what makes people stop, what keeps them watching and what makes them follow. So we stopped treating editing as decoration and made it our own craft, built on that knowledge. Today a young, fresh team that sharpens its skills every month turns raw footage into videos that stop the scroll.

Results

And the numbers say it works:

  • From zero: Divya went from 0 to 10K followers in 8 videos, and to 30K in 2 months
  • Multiplying an audience: 16 clips for The Third Lane Podcast brought 7.5M views and 30K new followers
  • Personal brands: Edits that connect with people, with 100M+ views across the brands we edit for
  • Startups: 2 startups pitched investors with our AI commercial
How we edit

Three rules.

Brand first

We learn what you expect before we cut. Every edit is aligned to your brand, not to a template.

Retention over decoration

Effects only stay if they keep people watching. Pacing and story come before polish.

Corrections in 24h

You review on Frame.io, comment on exact frames, and we fix it within the next 24 hours.
