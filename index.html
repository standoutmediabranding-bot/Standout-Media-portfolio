FAQ

Got questions? We got answers.

What types of videos do you edit?

Short-form reels, podcast clips, talking head videos, YouTube long form, AI commercial films, SaaS animation, motion graphics and thumbnails. Vertical and horizontal.

What do I need to send?

Your raw footage through a link or Drive, plus a short note on what you expect: the style, the platform and any reference videos.

How do corrections work?

We upload each edit to Frame.io. You leave comments on exact frames and we deliver the corrections within the next 24 hours.

Which tools do you use?

Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop, plus Canva for quick designs.

Can you edit for a startup pitch or product video?

Yes. Our AI commercials were used by two startups in their funding pitch, and we have made SaaS animations for fundraising and for Venzo Tech.
